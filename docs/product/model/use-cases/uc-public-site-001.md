## Goal

The reader understands ProductShape's purpose, process and artifacts and can see how the parts relate.

## Trigger

The reader opens the ProductShape public site.

## Preconditions

- The public site is available.

## Main Flow

1. The reader learns what ProductShape is and what it helps its users do.
2. The reader explores explanations of the process and its artifacts.
3. The reader uses diagrams and accompanying text to understand how the artifacts relate.
4. The reader follows links to further information or to a relevant engineering output.

## Alternative Flows

- The reader arrives directly at an explanation or diagram and can navigate to the broader product overview.

## Failure Conditions

- If a linked engineering output is unavailable, the site says so or omits the unavailable destination instead of implying that it exists.

## Postconditions

- The reader can explain ProductShape's purpose and recognize that diagrams are explanatory views, not canonical sources.
