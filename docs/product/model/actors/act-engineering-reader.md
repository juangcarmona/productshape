## Purpose

The ProductShape Engineering Reader wants to understand how ProductShape is structured and how its engineering work is evidenced, using published outputs without joining the repository workflow.

## Goals

- Find ProductShape's published Product Snapshot, Architecture Snapshot and available E2E journey evidence.
- Understand which source and revision or run each output represents.
- Navigate from a generated output to its relevant canonical source.

## Responsibilities

- Read and follow the engineering portal's published links.
- Treat snapshots and reports as derived, read-only outputs.

## Boundaries

- Does not author or approve ProductShape model or architecture documents through the portal.
- Does not assume an output exists until it is listed as available.
