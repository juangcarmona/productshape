## Purpose

The ProductShape Public Reader wants to understand what ProductShape is, how its process works and how its artifacts fit together.

## Goals

- Learn what ProductShape helps people do.
- Understand the product-definition and engineering workflow at a useful level.
- Explore clear explanations and diagrams without needing repository access.

## Responsibilities

- Use the public site to learn about ProductShape and follow its links to further information.

## Boundaries

- Does not treat explanatory diagrams as canonical product or architecture sources.
- Does not author, change or approve ProductShape artifacts through the public site.
