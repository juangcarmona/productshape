## Purpose

A person who wants to share a long web address in a short, reliable form.

## Goals

Turn a long URL into a short one and trust that it keeps working.

## Responsibilities

Provides the URL to shorten; distributes the short link.

## Boundaries

Does not administer the service or see other visitors' links.
