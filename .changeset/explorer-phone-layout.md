The Product Explorer is usable on a phone. Below 40rem the header keeps the title, search and a menu button, and the views, business lens and appearance open beneath it on request. Text and controls grow to a comfortable reading and touch size, and the maps keep their pixel scale. A page opened on a phone without an address starts on the artifact catalog, and every address, including `#/` for the Overview, is honoured. The map summary card can be folded to its title so it does not hide the map.
